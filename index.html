About Us

Empowering Farmers,
Growing Communities

Founded in 2005, GreenHarvest Agri has grown from a local seed distributor into a full-service agricultural solutions provider. We partner with farmers, cooperatives, and agri-businesses across 15 states to deliver the inputs, technology, and knowledge they need to thrive.

Our mission is simple: make sustainable, profitable farming accessible to everyone — from smallholder growers to large commercial operations.
